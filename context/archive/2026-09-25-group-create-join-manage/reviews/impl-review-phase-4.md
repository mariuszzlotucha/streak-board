## Findings

### F1 — Confirmation dialog overflows its card at 375 px for long emails and group names

- **Severity**: ⚠️ WARNING
- **Impact**: 🏃 LOW — quick decision; fix is obvious and narrowly scoped
- **Dimension**: Success Criteria
- **Location**: src/components/groups/ConfirmAction.tsx:66 (content from src/pages/dashboard.astro:121, 162 and, from Phase 3, :183)
- **Detail**: `AlertDialogContent` is a `grid` with `max-w-xs` (320 px) and an implicit `auto` column (src/components/ui/alert-dialog.tsx:46). The description only has `break-words` (`overflow-wrap: break-word`), which does not reduce the min-content width, so one long unbreakable token widens the column and the header, description, footer and both buttons are pushed past the card. Measured in headless Chrome at 375 px on the built preview: a member email of 44 characters without a space or hyphen (`firstname.lastname.dept1234@corp.example.com`) puts the content at 52-360 px inside a card spanning 28-348 px (the buttons stick out 12 px; the description is 36 px past its padding box); a 93-character email overflows by 9 px; an 80-character group name without spaces makes the content about 480 px wide (right edge near 530 px on a 375 px viewport, so the confirm button is partly off-screen) in the new Delete dialog and in the Phase 3 Leave dialog. A typical 33-character email fits, which is why manual check 4.8 ("usable at 375 px") and the plan's end state passed; the HTTP smoke cannot see a client-side dialog. Verified fix: setting `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on the description at runtime leaves 0 overflowing elements in every case above.
- **Fix**: In ConfirmAction.tsx:66 replace `break-words` with `wrap-anywhere` (Tailwind 4.3.3 utility for `overflow-wrap: anywhere`).
- **Decision**: FIXED via the proposed fix — `src/components/groups/ConfirmAction.tsx:66`: `break-words` → `wrap-anywhere` on the dialog description (unstaged on `s-01/group-create-join-manage/phase-4`).

### F3 — Smoke cannot see three properties of the destructive routes

- **Severity**: 💡 OBSERVATION
- **Impact**: 🔎 MEDIUM — real tradeoff; pause to reason through it
- **Dimension**: Success Criteria
- **Location**: scripts/smoke.mjs:370-396 (owner controls), :450-474 (removal), :183-188 (the only foreign-Origin step)
- **Detail**: The new steps are strong (exact redirects, page state, the target id read from the rendered control and cross-checked against B's session; the PR reports 8 deliberate mutations, each turning a step red), but three properties of this phase can still regress with the smoke green (accepted lesson: smoke steps must assert outcomes). (1) Confirmation: the dialog is not server-rendered, so `formPostingTo("/api/groups/delete")` stays green if `<ConfirmAction>` is replaced by a plain `<form method="POST" action=…><button type="submit">`, i.e. a one-click destructive action. (2) Scope of the removal: the group only ever has one non-owner (B), so "removes exactly the target" cannot be told apart from "removes every non-owner" (`.neq("user_id", user.id)` in place of `.eq("user_id", userId)` stays green). (3) Boundary: the only foreign-Origin step targets `create`; nothing asserts 403 for `remove-member` or `delete`, or that `GET` on them is rejected (a future `export const GET = POST` would stay green; safe methods are exempt from `checkOrigin` and SameSite=Lax cookies travel on cross-site top-level GETs). Today's behaviour is right: as non-owner B a foreign-Origin form POST answers 403 and `GET` answers 404 on both routes.
- **Fix**: Extend the smoke steps: (a) on the owner-controls step also assert the Radix trigger next to the destructive forms and that no `type="submit"` sits inside them; (b) add a third user C who joins A's group and assert that C still sees the group after A removes B and that B cannot remove C; (c) as B (non-owner, so side-effect free) assert that a foreign-Origin POST to `delete` and to `remove-member` answers 403 and that `GET` on both answers 404.
  - Strength: (a) was checked against the served HTML: `/<button[^>]*aria-haspopup="dialog"[^>]*>\s*Delete group\s*</` matches the real trigger and fails once it is swapped for a plain submit button, and `/<form[^>]*action="\/api\/groups\/(?:delete|remove-member)"[^>]*>(?:(?!<\/form>)[\s\S])*type="submit"/` is false on the real markup and true on a mutated copy. (c) was executed against the running preview (403/403/404/404). (b) turns the "exactly the target" outcome into something the smoke can observe.
  - Tradeoff: (a) couples the smoke to two markup details (`aria-haspopup`, `type="submit"`); (b) adds a sign-up, sign-in and join (three requests) per run.
  - Confidence: HIGH for (a) and (c), executed against real output; MED for (b), whose need follows from reading the steps but which was not run.
  - Blind spot: not break-checked with deliberate mutations the way Phase 3 F1 was; the dialog itself (Cancel/Esc/Confirm) stays a manual concern because the plan excludes an e2e runner (verified by hand in headless Chrome in this review).
- **Decision**: FIXED via the proposed fix — `scripts/smoke.mjs` (66 → 76 steps, unstaged on `s-01/group-create-join-manage/phase-4`): new helpers `rowWithConfirmedRemoveControl`, `removeTargetInRow`, `dialogTrigger`, `SUBMIT_IN_DESTRUCTIVE_FORM` and a `FOREIGN_ORIGIN` constant; (a) the owner-controls step now also requires the dialog trigger on every remove control and on the Delete card and forbids a `type="submit"` inside a destructive form; (b) a third user C (sign-up leaves a session, so no extra sign-in) joins, cannot be removed by non-owner B, still has the group after A removes B and loses it with the deletion, and the owner-side steps count 3 → 2 members; (c) as non-owner B a foreign-Origin POST answers 403 and `GET` answers 404 on `remove-member` and `delete`. `README.md:208` now says three signed-up users and lists foreign-origin/GET attempts. Verified: `prettier` and `eslint` clean, 76/76 on a fresh build. Break-checked with three temporary production-code mutations in one build (sources restored, identical to HEAD): the Delete control replaced by a plain `<form><button type="submit">` turned the owner-controls step red; `.neq("user_id", user.id)` in place of `.eq("user_id", userId)` turned "owner dashboard lists the owner and user C after removing user B" and "user C still has the group after user B was removed" red; `export const GET = POST` on both routes turned both GET steps red (302 instead of 404).

### F4 — A stale or double "Remove" ends on a false "not allowed" alert

- **Severity**: 💡 OBSERVATION
- **Impact**: 🏃 LOW — quick decision; fix is obvious and narrowly scoped
- **Dimension**: Safety & Quality
- **Location**: src/pages/api/groups/remove-member.ts:30-33, 46-48
- **Detail**: Same class as Phase 3 F3, which was fixed for `leave.ts` and, in this phase, for `delete.ts:21-25`, but not applied here. The plan maps every empty result to `forbidden`, so when the target is already gone the owner is told "You are not allowed to do that." although nothing is wrong. Reproduced on the built preview: (1) the owner removes B twice (a double click) → 302 `/dashboard`, then `/dashboard?error=forbidden`; (2) B leaves, then the owner's stale page clicks Remove → `?error=forbidden`; (3) the group is deleted in another tab, then Remove → `getMyGroup` returns null → `?error=forbidden` (the sibling `delete.ts` answers silently in exactly this case).
- **Fix**: In `remove-member.ts` redirect silently to `/dashboard` when `getMyGroup` returns null and, for the owner (`group.owner_id === user.id`), when the delete returns no row (the target is then simply not in the group any more); keep `forbidden` for a non-owner; add smoke steps for both (this relaxes the plan's literal "empty result → forbidden" for the owner case only).
- **Decision**: FIXED via the proposed fix — `src/pages/api/groups/remove-member.ts`: a caller without a group and an owner whose delete matched no row are redirected silently to `/dashboard`; a non-owner still gets `?error=forbidden` (unstaged on `s-01/group-create-join-manage/phase-4`). `scripts/smoke.mjs` (76 → 78 steps): "removing an already removed member is not an error" and "removing a member after the group is gone is not an error" (both exact `302 /dashboard`); the existing non-owner steps keep asserting `?error=forbidden`. The pre-fix behaviour was reproduced with HTTP probes before the change (double remove, B left first, group deleted first: all `?error=forbidden`). `prettier` and `eslint` clean.

### F5 — The self-removal guard rests on string equality after regex normalisation

- **Severity**: 💡 OBSERVATION
- **Impact**: 🏃 LOW — quick decision; fix is obvious and narrowly scoped
- **Dimension**: Safety & Quality
- **Location**: src/pages/api/groups/remove-member.ts:20; src/lib/group-rules.ts:38-45
- **Detail**: The invariant "a non-owner cannot leave through `remove-member`" (otherwise `group_members_delete_self` deletes their row) holds only because `normalizeUuid` accepts nothing but the canonical hyphenated form. Postgres accepts the braced, hyphenless and grouped spellings of a uuid (verified in the local database) and the regex rejects them today (verified), but if `UUID_PATTERN` is ever loosened, a non-owner posting their own id in another spelling passes the string comparison and leaves through the route (answer: a plain `/dashboard`) with every smoke step still green. The JSDoc at group-rules.ts:38-41 ("anything else would … fail with 22P02") is wrong for those spellings and hides why the strictness matters.
- **Fix**: Add `.neq("user_id", user.id)` to the delete in `remove-member.ts:37-42` so Postgres compares canonical uuids, add a smoke step posting B's own id without hyphens (→ `forbidden`), and correct the JSDoc.
- **Decision**: FIXED via the proposed fix — `src/pages/api/groups/remove-member.ts`: `.neq("user_id", user.id)` added to the delete (comment extended), so Postgres compares canonical uuids; `src/lib/group-rules.ts:38-42`: the JSDoc now says that Postgres also reads braced and hyphenless uuids and that this is why only the canonical spelling is accepted; `scripts/smoke.mjs` (78 → 79 steps): "remove-member of oneself without hyphens is refused" (`?error=forbidden`) (unstaged on `s-01/group-create-join-manage/phase-4`). Verified red/green with temporary mutations (sources restored): `UUID_PATTERN` loosened to accept hyphenless ids and the `.neq` taken out → the new step went red (`302 /dashboard`: B had left through the route; two cascade steps followed); the same loosened regex with the `.neq` kept → smoke 79/79. `prettier`, `eslint` and `astro check` clean.
