## Research Question

The roadmap leaves S-20 one unknown: "Co dokładnie jest nieczytelne na telefonie: liczba okresów, szerokość kolumny nicku, czy przewijanie w poziomie? Owner: user (zrzut ekranu z telefonu)" (`context/foundation/roadmap.md:83`), and the backlog says "Najpierw zrzut ekranu od właściciela" (`:131`). The repo holds no phone screenshot of the grid (see Historical Context), so this research measured the grid itself in the kitchen sink at phone widths, and collected the contracts a layout change has to keep: PRD, smoke, e2e, tokens and ESLint, the kitchen-sink gate, and the files S-21 and S-22 share with S-20.

## Summary

- **All three suspects are real, and they have different sizes.** Measured on `/dev/dashboard-kitchen-sink` (states `owner`, `member`, `long-text`; `astro dev` at 562081b; Playwright Chromium; dev toolbar hidden):
  1. **The grid is wider than the room it has, at each measured phone width from 320 to 412 px.** The room is `viewport − 80 px` (page `px-4`, `src/layouts/AppLayout.astro:51`; card `--card-spacing` 24 px per side, `src/components/ui/card.tsx:10`, `:56`): 240 px at 320, 280 at 360, 295 at 375, 313 at 393, 332 at 412, 350 at 430 (measured `li.clientWidth` at each width). The daily grid is 339–348 px wide with the fixture nicknames and 395 px with the 24-character one; the weekly grid 348–389 px (436 with the long nickname); both are content-sized and the same at every viewport (table 2). So in `owner` and `member` the daily and weekly grids scroll inside their wrapper at 320, 360, 375, 393 and 412 px; at 430 the daily grid fits (348 < 350) and the weekly one does not (389). **The column that is hidden is the current period**, the one the viewer acts on: at 375 px in `owner` the daily "Su 11" column lies at x = 352–384 against a visible edge of 335, and the weekly "from 5 Oct" column (right edge 425) is entirely off-screen. No scroll affordance is rendered, and the headless screenshots paint no scrollbar (`screenshots/research-owner-daily-row-375.png`, `research-owner-weekly-row-375.png`); phone browsers paint overlay scrollbars only during a scroll.
  2. **The page itself scrolls sideways**, which the S-13 contract excluded ("never the page", `context/archive/2026-10-17-checkoff-grid/plan.md:170`): `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` is 392 at a 375 px viewport in `owner`, 439 in `long-text`, and 360 at 320 px in `member` (375, 412 and 393 px in `member`/`owner` at the other widths stay at the viewport). The cause is the cells' screen-reader labels: `<span className="sr-only">` (`src/components/tasks/GridCell.tsx:29`) is `position: absolute`, and, measured for the first cell's label on each page (the markup is the same for every cell), no ancestor up to `html` is positioned (the wrapper `div.max-w-full.overflow-x-auto`, `CheckoffGrid.astro:22`, computes `position: static`), so the labels of the off-screen cells escape the wrapper's clipping and widen the document. Two counterfactuals confirm it: with `position: static` on those spans the page is 375/375/320 wide, and with `position: relative` on the wrapper it is 375/375/320 as well.
  3. **A long nickname covers the first columns instead of pushing them.** The row header is `max-w-32 pr-2` (128 px, `CheckoffGrid.astro:42`) and the column does stop at 128 px for 20-, 30- and 40-character names (experiment, table 3). But `truncate` sits on an inner span inside an `inline-flex` that has no width of its own (`:43-44`), so the 24-character fixture "Bartholomew_Featherstone" is not cut: its text runs over the "Mo 5" cell and the "You" badge sits over "Tu 6" (`screenshots/research-long-nickname-row-375.png`). FR-010 allows 2–24 characters counted as code points (`context/foundation/prd.md:61`, `src/lib/nickname-rules.ts:5`, `:99`), so a name in a wide script renders wider still: the second measurement (below) found 24 × "W" at 288 px of text, legal under FR-010, painting over four cells. Chromium only: CSS 2.2 §10.4 leaves `max-width` on table cells undefined, so WebKit (Safari and most iPhone browsers) may let the column grow to the name's width instead of capping it (no WebKit build in `~/.cache/ms-playwright`, so not measured).
- **What sets the width.** Cells are 28 px squares (`size-7`, `GridCell.tsx:27`) with 4 px `border-spacing-1` between columns and at both table edges (`CheckoffGrid.astro:23`), but the header labels decide the column: at 12 px (`text-xs`) in Outfit the daily labels "Mo 5"…"Su 11" measure 30–37 px (sum 231 for the fixture week) and the weekly "from 14 Sep"…"from 5 Oct" 67–74 px (sum 284); the nickname column is 32 px for "Sam", 40 for "Olivia", 72 for "Max" + You, 81 for "Olivia" + You, so the badge with its gap adds 40–41 px. Check: 81 + 231 + 9 × 4 = 348 (daily, `owner`); 81 + 284 + 6 × 4 = 389 (weekly, `owner`). The fixture week (days 5–11) is a narrow one: over the weekday and day-number labels the second measurement enumerated, the daily label text measures 19 px ("Fr 1") to 34 px ("Mo 20", "Mo 30", "We 20") in Outfit 400 at 12 px, so a period column is 28–42 px and seven of them 231 px (this week) to at most 294 px; a mid-month week such as 13–19 measures 252 px (second measurement).
- **Budget for the plan.** If header labels were no wider than the cells, a daily grid needs 7 × 28 + 9 × 4 = 232 px, leaving 63 px for the name column at 375 px, 48 at 360 and 100 at 412; a weekly grid needs 136 px, leaving 159 at 375. With today's labels the daily grid needs 267 px before the name column in the fixture week and up to 330 px in the widest week (28 px, or nothing, left at 375: less than the badge alone) and the weekly 308 px (more than the 295 px available with no name column at all). At 2 px spacing (`border-spacing-0.5`) seven 28 px cells need 214 px, leaving 81 px for the name at 375 and 66 at 360; a `w-full table-fixed` table at 375 with an 80 px name column gives (295 − 36 − 80) / 7 = 25.6 px per period column, below the 28 px cell, so that shape needs `size-6` cells or the 2 px spacing. The levers, in order of pixels: the header label format (`periodHeader`, `src/lib/grid-labels.ts:19-23`), the name column (badge, truncation or wrapping), and the 80 px of page and card padding.
- **Everything else in the row fits.** In `owner`, `member` and `long-text` at 360 and 375 px the only child of a task `<li>` whose `scrollWidth` exceeds its `clientWidth` is the grid wrapper (probe over the first seven rows of each state).
- **Contracts a layout change must keep**: the horizon 7 / 4 / 1 columns and the four cell states are PRD FR-013 (`prd.md:123`), so fewer columns is a PRD change, not a layout fix; smoke's row regexes stop at `<table` and `taskRowGridCell` needs `<table aria-label="Check-offs of …">` with the label `Nick, period: state</span>` in the row's last `<td>` (`scripts/smoke.mjs:220-235`, `:361-367`); the e2e round trip finds the grid by `getByRole("table", { name })` and the same label (`tests/e2e/checkoff-round-trip.spec.ts:34-36`) and runs on Pixel 7 at 412 px (`playwright.config.ts:34-38`); cells may use only the token pairs `scripts/token-contrast.mjs` checks (`:21`, `:23`, `:27`); ESLint rejects literal colours and arbitrary px/rem in `src/components/{dashboard,tasks}/` (CLAUDE.md, UI); the kitchen sink and `scripts/ui-screenshots.mjs` (1280×800 and 375×812, no custom width, `:19-22`, `:444-445`) are the visual gate.
- **No earlier evidence from a phone exists in the repo or in the PR bodies searched (#169, #174, #202, #203).** Row 3.6 of S-13 ("readable at 375 px width without page scroll") was ticked in one batch with four other manual rows and no detail, no S-13 screenshot was committed, and the deployment plan has no S-13 phase (Historical Context).

### The page scrolls sideways: the `sr-only` escape

- Measured `document.documentElement.scrollWidth`: `owner` 392 at 320, 360, 375 and 390 px, 412 at 412, 393 at 393, 430 at 430; `long-text` 439 at 320–430; `member` 360 at 320, then equal to the viewport at 360–430. S-13 did not measure it: row 3.6 was ticked without a note and no screenshot was committed (Historical Context).
- Cause, measured: the cell label `<span className="sr-only">` (`GridCell.tsx:29`) computes `position: absolute; width: 1px; white-space: nowrap`; its containing block is the initial containing block, because the nearest positioned ancestor search from the span up to `html` finds none (the wrapper is `static`, so is the `<td>`'s span, the `<li>`, the card). Per CSS 2.2 §11.1.1 an `overflow` value clips an element's content except descendants whose containing block is the viewport or an ancestor of that element, so these spans sit at their static position (inside the off-screen cell) in page coordinates and extend the scrollable overflow of the document: the span "Olivia, week from 5 Oct: done" has `right` = 392 in `owner`, exactly the page's `scrollWidth`.
- Counterfactuals, measured on the same pages: `table td .sr-only { position: static }` → page 375 / 375 / 320; `div.overflow-x-auto { position: relative }` → page 375 / 375 / 320. Either one-class change removes the page scroll; the plan picks (the wrapper `relative` keeps the labels screen-reader-only without touching `GridCell.tsx`, which S-21 edits).
- Why 375 px in `member` does not overflow: with the name column at 72 px ("Max" + You) the widest table is 348 and its last label's static position lands at x ≤ 375 (measured page width 375); at 320 it does not (360). So the overflow depends on the nickname widths of the real group, not on the fixtures alone.

### Column anatomy and the width budget

- Cell: `span.mx-auto.flex.size-7.items-center.justify-center.rounded-md` with a `size-4` lucide icon (`GridCell.tsx:27-28`): 28 × 28 px, measured 28 px high in every row of the three states. `<td class="p-0">` (`CheckoffGrid.astro:51`).
- Header: `th.px-1.font-normal.whitespace-nowrap.text-muted-foreground` (`:31`) with `periodHeader` text (`grid-labels.ts:19-23`: "Mo 13", "from 13 Oct", "Done"). Measured widths at `text-xs` (12 px, font "Outfit Variable"): "Mo 5" 34, "Tu 6" 31, "We 7" 34, "Th 8" 33, "Fr 9" 30, "Sa 10" 37, "Su 11" 32 (sum 231); "from 14 Sep" 72, "from 21 Sep" 71, "from 28 Sep" 74, "from 5 Oct" 67 (sum 284); "Done" 37. A period column is max(label + 8, 28) px: each header of the fixture week is wider than the 28 px cell, and over the enumerated labels only the narrowest ("Fr 1", 19 px of text) falls under it, so the header, not the cell, sets the column in almost every week.
- Spacing: `border-separate border-spacing-1` (`:23`) is 4 px between columns and at both edges: 9 gaps (36 px) for 8 columns, 6 gaps (24 px) for 5, 3 gaps (12 px) for 2. Arithmetic check against the measurements: daily `owner` 81 + 231 + 36 = 348; weekly `owner` 81 + 284 + 24 = 389; daily one-row 32 + 231 + 36 = 299; once `owner` 81 + 37 + 12 = 130.
- Name column (`th.max-w-32.pr-2`, 8 px right padding): "Sam" 32, "Olivia" 40, "Max" + You 72, "Olivia" + You 81, cap 128. The badge (`src/components/ui/badge.tsx:7`, `h-5 px-2 text-xs whitespace-nowrap`; measured 37 px) plus the `gap-1` adds 40–41 px (81 − 40; 72 − 32 for a three-letter name). Why `truncate` never fires: `overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis; white-space: nowrap` needs a box with a bounded width, but the inner span's parent `inline-flex` shrink-wraps to its content and is itself no flex item, so its `min-w-0` (`CheckoffGrid.astro:43`) does nothing, and the `th`'s `max-w-32` bounds the column without clipping (`overflow: visible`).
- Budget (room from table 1; arithmetic from the numbers above):
  - Daily with headers no wider than the cells: 7 × 28 + 9 × 4 = 232 px → 63 px left for the name column at 375, 48 at 360, 8 at 320, 100 at 412. Today's "Olivia" + You needs 81 and "Max" + You 72; a 6-letter name without the badge needs 40.
  - Daily with today's labels: 231 + 36 = 267 px → 28 px left at 375, 13 at 360: no name column fits, not even "Sam" (32).
  - Weekly with 28 px headers: 4 × 28 + 6 × 4 = 136 px → 159 px left at 375. Weekly with today's labels: 284 + 24 = 308 px > 295: nothing fits at 375 even with an empty name column.
  - The chrome: card padding is `--spacing(6)` = 24 px per side, and the card's `data-size=sm` variant is `--spacing(4)` = 16 px (`card.tsx:10`); the page padding is `px-4` (`AppLayout.astro:51`). Shrinking the card padding on phones gains 16 px, bleeding the grid out of the card padding gains 48 px; both change what every other card or row looks like at that width.

### Contracts that bind a layout change

- **PRD**: FR-013 fixes the horizon (7 daily, 4 weekly, 1 once) and the four cell states (`prd.md:123`; owner decision 2026-10-17, `context/archive/2026-10-17-checkoff-grid/frame.md:41`), with the counter-argument "siatka to najszerszy widok w aplikacji" recorded and kept (`:124-126`); the NFR is full usability on a smartphone without loss of function against a larger screen (`:130`); the check-off guardrail is instant feedback (`:40`). Fewer visible periods, a swipe-only history or a collapsed grid is a PRD change.
- **Roadmap**: S-20's outcome is "bez poziomego przewijania; uczestnicy i okresy są czytelne, a długi nick nie wypycha kolumn" (`roadmap.md:76`), MS-01 adds "nazwisko/nick nie zasłania kolumny" (`:29`); S-13's contract was the opposite choice, "Horizontal overflow scrolls inside the table wrapper, never the page" (`2026-10-17-checkoff-grid/plan.md:170`; comment at `CheckoffGrid.astro:20-21`), so S-20 reverses a deliberate decision rather than fixing an accident. Risk line: "zmiana układu nie może zepsuć widoku komputerowego ani kitchen sinka (`scripts/smoke.mjs` wiąże markup siatki)" (`:84`).
- **Smoke** (`scripts/smoke.mjs`, regexes over the server HTML): `taskRowListing` (`:220-226`) and `taskRowWithBadge` (`:361-367`, used by `taskRowWithStatus` `:271-273`) stop at the first `<table` because the grid repeats the nicknames, the "You" badge and the "Done" header; `taskRowGridCell` (`:228-235`) requires `<table[^>]*aria-label="Check-offs of <title>"`, the label `<nick>, <period without < or :>: <state></span>` and that span inside the row's last `<td>` before `</tr>`; each of the other seven row helpers (`:240`, `:247`, `:254`, `:266`, `:280`, `:377`, `:384`) tolerates no `</li>` inside the row. The kitchen-sink probe needs `?state=owner` to answer 200 with "Olivia" in dev and 404 in production (`:776-781`). Consequences: nickname or badge markup placed before the `<table` tag, a second `<table>` per row, or a `<ul>`/`<li>` grid breaks the suite; a CSS-grid of `<div>`s instead of `<table>` breaks `taskRowGridCell` and the e2e locator.
- **E2E**: `tests/e2e/checkoff-round-trip.spec.ts:26` finds the row by `getByRole("listitem").filter({ hasText })` and `:34-36` the cell by `getByRole("table", { name: "Check-offs of …" }).getByText(/^nick, [^:]+: state$/)`, asserted `toBeAttached` (the labels are `sr-only`); every spec runs on `chromium` (Desktop Chrome) and `mobile-chromium` (Pixel 7, 412×839) (`playwright.config.ts:28-39`). ## Architecture Insights

- The grid is content-sized (`table-layout: auto`, no `w-full`), so its width is a pure function of the labels, the nicknames and the spacing; a layout that must fit a given width has to bound every one of the three or switch the table to `table-fixed`/`w-full` with wrapping or truncating headers.
- Period labels are produced in one pure module (`grid-labels.ts`) that is unit-testable with fixed keys; the visible header (`periodHeader`) and the screen-reader name (`periodName`) are separate functions, so the header can get shorter without changing the labels smoke and e2e bind.
- `sr-only` on a descendant of a scrolling container needs a positioned container (or the labels as `static` visually-hidden text); the same pattern (absolute `sr-only` inside an `overflow-x-auto` box with no positioned ancestor) widens the page wherever the hidden text sits past the viewport.
- Phone checks in this repo are manual (375 px screenshots, kitchen sink) and the e2e phone project asserts function only (`playwright.config.ts:34-35`, `test-plan.md:164-165`, `:221`); an automated "no horizontal page scroll" assertion does not exist and would be new.
- Tailwind spacing-scale utilities (`max-w-32`, `size-7`) are the lint-clean way to write widths in the dashboard components (CLAUDE.md "No literals"); a width the scale does not offer needs a token in `global.css` with `tokens-source/` and the contrast script, or a `%`/`calc()`/`var()` value, which review decides on.

## Open Questions

For `/10x-plan`, none blocking the plan; the first two are product choices for the owner.

1. **Target widths.** The roadmap says "bez poziomego przewijania" (`roadmap.md:76`) without a width. Which must show all 7 (4) columns with no in-wrapper scroll: 360 px (common Android), 375 px (e.g. iPhone SE 2–3 and 13 mini, the repo's manual check), 412 px (the e2e phone project), 320 px (first-generation SE: 240 px of room, 8 px left for names even with 28 px columns)? The owner's phone is an Android of unknown width (`deployment-plan.md:438`).
2. **The name column.** Three shapes fit the budget: (a) truncate at a fixed width with an ellipsis (needs the `truncate` on a block with a width, e.g. the `th` itself, and loses the end of similar names); (b) wrap with `wrap-anywhere` over two lines (row height 28 → 56 px, the S-01 fix); (c) take the name out of the column (a name line above its cells, or the badge dropped and "You" shown another way). Shape (c) changes the table's row-header semantics; smoke and e2e keep working only while the label spans stay inside the `<table>`'s last `<td>` per row.
3. **Header labels.** The largest lever: daily "Mo 5" → a weekday initial over the day number (two lines, ≤ 28 px) or the day number alone; weekly "from 14 Sep" → "14 Sep", "14.09" or a week number. `periodHeader` (`grid-labels.ts:19-23`) changes alone; `periodName` (the bound labels) stays. S-13 chose the current texts in its contract (`plan.md:170`).
4. **Chrome.** Whether phones get a smaller card padding (`data-size=sm`, 16 px) for every card, or only the grid bleeds out of the padding; either way the kitchen-sink `states` set is the gate and `scripts/ui-screenshots.mjs` has no 360 or 412 px viewport today.
5. **Page scroll fix and its check.** `position: relative` on the wrapper (`CheckoffGrid.astro:22`) removes the page scroll; whether S-20 adds an automated check (an e2e assertion on `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= innerWidth` on `mobile-chromium`, or a check in `ui-screenshots.mjs`), since none exists.
6. **Real device confirmation.** The fixtures' widest case is a 24-character Latin name; the production group's nicknames and the owner's device width are unknown. The plan's manual rows should take the owner's phone screenshot before and after, as the roadmap asked (`:131`).
7. **Shared files.** If S-20 changes the cell's outer span (size or spacing in `GridCell.tsx:27`), S-21 rebases on it; if it leaves `GridCell.tsx` and `ViewerGridCell.tsx` alone, S-21 and S-22 stay independent.
8. **Browsers and text size.** Every number here is Chromium's at 100 % text scaling. The absolute-position escape is specified behaviour (CSS 2.2 §11.1.1), so every engine shares it; the `max-width` cap on the `th` is undefined by CSS 2.2 §10.4, so WebKit (Safari and most iPhone browsers) may let the name column grow instead.
